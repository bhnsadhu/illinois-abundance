The section introduction identifies Illinois Abundance as the curator. Readers visit the publisher for the full reporting. The three current entries use original titles and summaries and contain no reproduced photographs or article excerpts.

This is an editorial approach based on the distinction between facts and protected expression, not a publisher license or a blanket legal clearance. The [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html) explains that copyright protects expression rather than facts or ideas. Its [fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html) says there is no fixed safe number of words or percentage. Attribution alone does not grant permission to reproduce protected material.

## Editing and future expansion

Read the source, verify its date, and write a concise factual description in fresh wording. Avoid close paraphrases, copied ledes, quotations, and descriptions that substitute for reading the original. Credit the publication visibly and link to the specific article, not its homepage. Keep the source's publication date distinct from the date we selected it. Remove or correct broken, withdrawn, or inaccurate entries.

No scraping or automatic feed import is implemented. Public availability of a page or RSS feed does not establish republication rights. Before adding automation or licensed excerpts, review that publisher's feed/API terms and record the permission or license, allowed fields and lengths, required author/publication credit, canonical link, image rights, and any expiry or revocation conditions. Follow any license-specific attribution requirements. No publishers have been contacted and no republication permissions have been obtained in this review.
